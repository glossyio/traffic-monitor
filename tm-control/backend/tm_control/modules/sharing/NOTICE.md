# Sharing with Traffic Monitor's server

This notice covers what your device sends to Traffic Monitor's server (tb.roadwaybiome.com), run by Roadway Biome, and what is done with it. It doesn't cover a server you run yourself or any other server.

Sharing is off until you turn it on. Each choice below is separate, and you can turn each one off on this page at any time. The data on your device, and the dashboards on your device, stay yours whether you share or not.

## Share data

Your device sends its counts, speeds, radar and air-quality readings, and its system health (versions, temperature, disk use, its name on your network, and its serial number). For each object it counted, it also sends the path the object took across the camera's picture and the box around it, as numbers, never a picture, and how long the object stood still and how long it spent in each zone. Every five minutes it also sends whether each camera and radar was working, so that a quiet road can be told from a sensor that was off. It also sends the device's setup whenever it changes: which detection model is running (its fingerprint, type, and size, never its name), the software versions, and each camera's detection settings, including the shape of every mask and of every zone that is on, and the names you typed for those zones. For each camera and radar, it also sends how the sensor is mounted, where you recorded it: which way it faces, how high it is above the road, how far it tilts down, how far away the middle of its view is, and the kind of mount (a tripod, a pole, or a window). With them goes the location as your database stores it: rounded to about 1 km unless you chose otherwise on the Location page, or no location at all. Read more at [docs.trafficmonitor.ai](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai).

It doesn't send images, video, plate reads, the rest of your device's settings, your Wi-Fi details, or the descriptions you give places.

Roadway Biome uses the data to run the service and to study traffic. It may also use it:

- to offer dashboards and reports beyond what your device shows, for people such as advocates and traffic engineers. Some may be free and some may be part of a paid account; Roadway Biome intends to keep a free option.
- in advocacy for safer streets, where it may be combined with data from other devices in the same area.

What Roadway Biome makes public from this data is meant to be aggregated, not one device's readings on their own. The public map is the exception, and it is a separate choice below.

How the data is used is governed by [Roadway Biome's Terms of Service](https://www.roadwaybiome.com/terms), which take precedence over this summary.

## Share the exact location

Your device also sends the exact coordinates of the place it is at. Roadway Biome may use them to place the device accurately in its dashboards and reports (where your account has them), to work out which street block the device is on, and to run the service. Reports and advocacy may name that street block. The exact coordinates aren't published or given to anyone else.

Turning this off stops your device sending them. Exact locations already received stay on the server and are handled under the Terms of Service; to ask for them to be deleted, see below.

## Turning sharing off

Turning off "Share data" stops every upload. What the server already has stays there. To ask for it to be deleted, contact Roadway Biome at [info@roadwaybiome.com](mailto:info@roadwaybiome.com).

## Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, your device asks an administrator to read the new version. Sharing continues as you set it; anything new waits for your choice.

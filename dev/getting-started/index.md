# Choose your path

## 1. Get a Traffic Monitor

- **[Build your own](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/build-your-own-device-diy/index.md)**

  Buy the [hardware](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/build-your-own-device-diy/recommended-hardware/index.md), [assemble](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/build-your-own-device-diy/assembly-instructions/index.md) it, and [install the software](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/software-installation/index.md). - **Start with a pre-built unit**

  ______________________________________________________________________

  The software is installed. Skip to step 2, and ask your provider how the unit connects to your network.

## 2. Deploy it

The device needs a clear view of the roadway, and the radar needs a shallow angle to the direction of travel. [Choose a site and mount](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/deployment-and-mounting-guide/index.md) covers both, for [temporary](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/deployment-and-mounting-guide/#temporary-deployments) and [permanent](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/deployment-and-mounting-guide/#permanent-deployments) deployments.

## 3. Set it up

Power it on and follow [First-time setup](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/setup-guide/index.md): open the home page, turn on detection, adjust the zones to your roadway, and record the device's location. This takes about half an hour.

## 4. Use the data

The device counts around the clock and starts again by itself after a power cut. Watch the [Monitoring dashboard](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/ui/monitoring/index.md), review detections in Frigate, [export the data](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/ui/database/index.md), or send it to [ThingsBoard](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/data-and-payloads/integrations/index.md).

Some operators pair the device with a display that shows the day's counts. Share what you build in [Show and tell](https://github.com/glossyio/traffic-monitor/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell) or on the [Zulip chat](https://trafficmonitor.zulipchat.com/).

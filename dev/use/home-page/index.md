## description: The device's home page and the apps it links to: dashboards, Frigate, Node-RED, camera feeds, and Cockpit.

# Home page and apps

Open `http://<device>/` in a browser on the same network. `<device>` is the hostname you set when you imaged the card, followed by `.local` (for example `http://tm-01.local/`), or the device's IP address.

The home page is the device's [control app](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/control-app/index.md). A new device first asks you to claim it with its setup code; after that, the home page and every app ask you to log in. The home page shows the device's name, software version, serial number, and time, a one-line summary of its health, and links to the apps your account can open. Its menu has **Status**, **Device**, and **Date and time** (see [Check and fix the device](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/device-health/index.md)), **Detection** (see [Choose the detection model](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/detection/index.md)), **Location** for administrators (see [Places and location](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/location/index.md)), your **Account**, the **Users** page for administrators, and **Log out**.

Finding the device's address

- If you set a hostname in Raspberry Pi Imager, `http://<hostname>.local/` works from most computers and phones on the same network.
- Otherwise, look the device up in your router's list of connected devices, or see [Find the IP address of your Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/remote-access.html#ip-address).
- A pre-built unit may host its own Wi-Fi network. Connect to it and use the hostname or the gateway address. Your provider's instructions take precedence.

If the home page doesn't load

The apps accept connections only from the device, so they have no address of their own on your network. If the apps show a page saying the control app isn't answering, restart it as described in [If the control app is down](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/control-app/#if-the-control-app-is-down). If nothing loads, open Cockpit at `http://<device>:9090`, log in with your Raspberry Pi user account, and check the services as described in [System administration](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/use/system-admin/index.md). [Network ports](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/reference/network/index.md) lists what the device opens. If the home page loads on a computer but times out on an Android phone, see [Troubleshooting](https://docs.trafficmonitor.ai/dev/help-and-faq/frequently-asked-questions/#troubleshooting).
